Top bar

See collar status and open Controls to change recording, listening, AI and audio.

The OS row shows your phone’s time, network and battery. Compact icons on the left of the K9Link bar show collar status and open Controls. Two separate arrows at the far right switch profile screens directly.

Top bar · left to right

Settings
Edit dogs, profiles and app options.
Profiles
Choose another profile, such as Default or Training.
Connection
Open collar connection settings. Teal: online; red: offline; amber: paused or waiting.
Collar batteries
A named percentage for each dog. Last means the reading is stale; — means no reading. This is separate from your phone’s OS battery indicator.
Recording
In Controls, use the Recording switch to start or stop saving without another confirmation. Filled red: saving; outline: stopped. Live viewing and tracking continue.
Command listening
In Controls, use the Command listening switch for “Rex, sit”. Highlighted mic: listening; crossed-out mic: off. Running logic is not cancelled.
AIAI
In Controls, open AI to choose optional collar or phone video and press Start. Highlighted: active. Stop AI ends the session. Replies use the phone’s speaker or headphones.
Collar audio
First in Controls: On allows collar headphones and speaker playback. Off stops playback and blocks further collar audio. Recording, tracking and AI phone replies continue.
Previous screen
The separate left arrow at the far right goes to the previous screen in this profile. It is faded and disabled on the first screen.
Next screen
The separate right arrow goes to the next screen in this profile. It is faded and disabled on the last screen. The number between the arrows shows your position.
